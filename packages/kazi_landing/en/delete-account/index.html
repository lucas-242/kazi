Delete your Kazi account

You can erase your account and all of its data whenever you want. In the app, it happens on the spot. If you no longer have the app installed, ask us by e-mail.

In the app

  1. Open Kazi and tap Settings, in the bottom bar.
  2. Tap Delete account, at the end of the list.
  3. Confirm and sign in again with the same Google account.

The account and its data are erased on the spot, without waiting on anyone.

Without the app

Send the request from the e-mail address of the Google account you use in Kazi. That is how we confirm the request is yours. We erase everything within 30 days and reply once it is done.

Request deletion by e-mail

Or write directly to contact@kazipro.io

What is erased

  • The services you registered, with their amounts, dates and commissions.
  • Your clients and their contact details.
  • Your service catalog.
  • Your account settings, such as currency and pay cycle.
  • Access to Kazi through your Google account.

What is not erased

  • Your Kazi Pro subscription, if you have one. It is billed by Google Play or the App Store, and deleting the account does not cancel it: cancel it in the store's subscriptions.
  • Usage events and session recordings, which carry neither your name nor your amounts. Analytics providers keep them for a limited period and delete them automatically afterwards.
  • Crash reports, kept for up to 90 days.

The details of each item are in the privacy policy.
